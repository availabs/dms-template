A cell is the share of that hour's possible 5-minute epochs that were actually reported, averaged over the month's days. Overnight coverage is structurally lower everywhere on the network — few enough probe vehicles pass to fill every epoch — so the useful reading is the daytime band and any month that breaks the pattern.

Weekday and weekend are the first split: a Saturday afternoon hour is weekend, never off peak. Overnight wins over both — a 22:00 Saturday hour is overnight.

outliers

Filtering trims both tails of the raw epochs before binning. On a congested segment the slow tail is the finding, so 2σ is the wrong default — it removes 4.6% of observations, most of them the worst hours.
