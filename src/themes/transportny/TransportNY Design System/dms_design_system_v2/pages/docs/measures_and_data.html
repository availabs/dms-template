Common tasks

  • Check data coverage before you compare two years of anything.
  • Pick the right delay variant for a trend, a cross-class comparison or a federal check.
  • Quote a CY 2025 reliability figure with its year, system, target and basis.
  • Find out what "statewide" means when no region is selected.
  • Find out why a month or a region shows $0 or nothing.
  • Check whether a year is partial before quoting its total.
  • Check which value-of-time basis a page uses before quoting a dollar figure.
