Layer reference.

Every layer on the Freight Atlas map, category by category: what it shows, the dataset it draws, its legend labels and, where it has them, the views you can switch between. Written from the live map configuration, read 2026-09-05, and the catalog's dataset descriptions. The layer-side counterpart of Freight Atlas sources.

reference applies to · Freight Atlas (www/freightatlas) last reviewed · 2026-09-07 owner · —

How to read this page

A layer is one row in the map's Map layers panel: a styled drawing of one dataset, with a legend and the fields shown when you hover. The map has 39 layers in 8 categories. Layer is the row's name as published. What it shows comes from the layer's configuration and the dataset's description, with the data year where the description states one. Source is the dataset's catalog name, linked to its entry in the sources index; "not publicly readable" means the layer draws a dataset without a public catalog entry. Legend / filters gives the legend labels, and for the five layers with views, the Show options in order, first one default. Legend ranges are abbreviated as the map shows them (K thousand, M million, B billion). "No hover fields" marks the 13 layers that show no popup.

basisExtent is New York State unless the row says otherwise; several federal datasets cover the whole country and are drawn as delivered. Data years are the datasets' own vintages, not the upload dates. TRANSEARCH layers draw the 2021 base year of the 2023 release.

Layers that read the same dataset

Several layers are different drawings of one dataset, so their downloads are the same file. The three core rail layers, 2024 Core Rail Freight Network, Core Rail Freight Network by Class and STRACNET, all read NYS Freight Core Rail Network. Roads, New York State Thruway and Traffic Volume & Road Attributes all read New York HPMS. STCC Transearch Value and STCC Transearch Tonnage share one dataset, as do Transearch Totals and Transearch Total Value. Two layers read two datasets each: Pipeline System and Truck Parking Inventory Buffer. One readable Freight Atlas dataset, NY State Bridge Inventory, has no layer.

Names, suffixes and recent changes

  • The panel labels a row with the dataset drawing's own name, so two layers read differently from this page: Major Ports appears as Major Maritime Ports, and on the published map the two core rail layers carry "v2" and "v4". The suffixes distinguished drafts of the same drawing; they do not mean different data. The rename that drops them awaits publishing.
  • A legend block is titled with the inner layer name, which for STRAHNET, STRACNET, the core rail layers, NYC Truck Routes and the Transearch layers is the dataset's provenance name rather than the panel name. Match a block to a layer by its labels.
  • Nine layers were added in July 2026 to draw plan figures the map lacked: Freight Demand Intensity, Disadvantaged Populations (DAC Rank), Business Locations Density, Truck Crash Density, Top 10 Truck Bottlenecks, Truck Bottleneck Segments, Undesignated Truck Parking (ATRI), New York State Thruway and Transearch Total Value.
  • "Road Performance Measures" became Traffic Volume & Road Attributes on 2026-07-27 and moved from Safety & Performance to Road Network; a layer named "Freight Network Performance Measures" was removed in July 2026 as a duplicate.
  • Legend labels for the air cargo, port and terminal layers are the datasets' own upper-case names.

Vocabulary

AADT · truck AADT
Annual average daily traffic: the average number of vehicles a day on a road segment. The truck-only counts appear as AADT (Single Trucks) and AADT (Combination Trucks), single-unit and combination trucks; the FCHN popup shows them as AADT Single and AADT Combo.
ATRI
The American Transportation Research Institute, whose licensed truck-parking clusters the undesignated parking layer draws.
DAC rank
The percentile rank of a disadvantaged-communities index built from Environmental Protection Agency indicators of locational disadvantage, such as walkability, air pollution and commute length.
FCHN
The Freight Core Highway Network, New York's designated core highway network for freight, created in the 2019 plan and extended by 4,497 miles in the 2024 plan.
IRI
The International Roughness Index, a pavement roughness measure; the layer's hover label calls it a roughness indicator, and a higher value is a rougher road.
Last Mile
The third corridor type of the core rail network's legend, after National and Statewide, drawn light since July 2026. It is the third tier of the plan's core rail network, below National and Statewide, as the plan's Figure 39 labels it.
NHFN · PHFS · CUFC · CRFC
The National Highway Freight Network, the Federal Highway Administration's designated freight network, and its components: the Primary Highway Freight System, other Interstate portions not on the PHFS, Critical Urban Freight Corridors and Critical Rural Freight Corridors.
PADD
Petroleum Administration for Defense District, the Energy Information Administration's regional grouping shown in the terminals popup.
REDC
Regional Economic Development Council; the plan's maps use the REDC regions as their background.
STCC
Standard Transportation Commodity Code, the commodity classification TRANSEARCH uses; the Transearch layers filter by its top-level groups.
STRACNET
The Strategic Rail Corridor Network, the rail lines flagged in the core rail dataset; the plan's Figure 19 draws it with STRAHNET.
STRAHNET
The Strategic Highway Network, the highways designated for defence mobility, drawn as routes and connectors.
TRANSEARCH
S&P Global's commodity-flow database. The plan and the map use the 2023 release with a 2021 base year, written "TRANSEARCH 2021 base year (2023 release)".
