What is actually being shown when there is no filter on region? Is there anyway to clarify this? ticket 2191486

Statewide. No region selected means the full MAP-21 dataset, the same aggregate behind the federal figures. It is deliberately not the sum of the 11 regions. The dataset includes border and unassigned segments that belong to no region, so the regions add up to less. The Reliability filter bar now reads "no region selected = statewide", and clearing a region with its ✕ restores the statewide figures. The same rule holds on the delay and event dashboards. See What statewide means.

Why are we showing this on an annual basis for all years instead of the year selected???? ticket 2214490

Because the measure is annual. Level of Travel Time Reliability (LOTTR) is a federal MAP-21 measure defined on a calendar year. No monthly form exists in any data the platform holds. The delay series is monthly, so its cards follow the year; the reliability series cannot. The home's reliability card is labelled annual for that reason, and the Reliability trend shows every year whatever Year says. See Person-miles reliable by year.

it says "How it's computed ->" at the bottom. This is not clickable and there is no information on how it's computed. ticket 2191403 · ticket 2214480

That line, and a similar chip on the excessive-delay card, were static text styled like links. Both were removed, in July and August 2026. Where the method lives now: the Data & Methodology page in TSMO's Program group carries one explainer per measure, every formula variable expanded. This documentation has one page per measure. Start with Cost of congestion and Excessive delay.

Region 2, August 2025, Incidents costs are $0. running fix 2172498

Incident cost is the sum of per-event footprints, and a footprint exists on about 18.5 % of events. A region and month whose events carry no footprint shows $0. That is the shape Region 2 produced for August 2025. A month that has not been computed at all shows nothing rather than zero. See Why a month or a region shows $0 or nothing.

When you select drill into a corridor, it does not keep your regional selection. Corridor view switches to Queens county. ticket 2195006

The button was removed in July 2026. The Congestion dashboard filters by NYSDOT region. Corridor View is keyed by county, road and direction, and the network it reads carries no region, so the selection could never cross. Open Corridor View from Explorers and pick the county yourself. See Why the region selection does not carry into it.

Done on 2026-07-28, and the split was tightened at the same time. Incidents now excludes Construction, Maintenance and Emergency Operations, and its Category list offers only the seven reactive categories. Work Zones holds exactly those three, and its Type list offers only them. The two dashboards no longer overlap. Incident Search still searches every category. A map showing which corridor is selected would be helpful to verify exactly which segments you are looking at. ticket 2195605 · ticket 2195604

Three maps exist today. Corridor View has Corridor strip map, built in July 2026, which draws exactly the segments feeding the grid. Reliability has Where travel is unreliable, every segment coloured by worst-period LOTTR. The incident page has Location & affected segment. Two do not exist: the Congestion dashboard's segment delay map and the Incidents dashboard's cluster map, both marked by placeholder notes. The Work Zones active-zones map was removed as out of scope. See What this page does not show.

what is [the version label on the delay card]? is that supposed to be a link? Is it important to that boxes contents??? ticket 2214480 · ticket 2214495

It named the platform's internal version of the delay computation and has been removed from the home, with the "series" wording on the freshness card. It referred to the June 2026 revision of the excessive-delay series: thresholds from each segment's posted speed, median baselines, and event attribution capped at the non-recurrent total. Figures from before that revision are not comparable and should not be mixed with the current ones. See Comparability across years and versions.

Could we view these same statistics by county? (select one or more). This would be very helpful for MPOs ticket 2194997 · ticket 2214565

Not on the dashboards today: they filter by NYSDOT region only. A county view is feasible, because the delay data carries a county code. It needs a county filter and county-grouped panels, and is scoped as a feature. MPO geography does not exist anywhere in TSMO. Adding it needs MPO boundaries and a rule for segments that straddle them; no delivery date has been set. Today, county-level data is in Corridor View and the county facet of Incident Search. See Order, county view and MPO filtering.

The filter allows the user to select multiple years but the panels only show the data for the most recent year selected. ticket 2191408

Fixed in July 2026. Year is single-select on every dashboard, because the panels are single-year reads and multi-select only misled. Picking a year replaces the previous one. Incident Search keeps multi-select on its facets, because it is a query tool. See Using the filter bar.

By default the "region" and "Category" are blank but it looks like the data is representing the same thing as if you were to select all. ticket 2195366

Yes. A blank control is no filter: blank Region is statewide and blank Category is every reactive category. Since July 2026 the controls have a visible border at rest, Year always shows its value, and a chosen region carries a ✕. See Using the filter bar.

It says that this chart reacts to the region filter but for many regions the delay does not exceed this .1M threshold which results in missing years or a completely missing bar chart. ticket 2192555

Fixed in July 2026. The chart's values were pre-scaled to millions and rounded, so a region under about 50,000 vehicle-hours rounded to nothing. It now plots raw vehicle-hours with an axis that scales to the data: statewide reads in millions, a small region in thousands, both with full-height bars. See Incidents dashboard.

Delay Composition graph is mismarked or misleading ---- should grey area be "recurrent" congestion instead of "non-recurrent" congestion? ticket 2211340

Grey is recurrent, and always was. The legend entry read "recurrent (total − non-recurrent)", so the swatch sat next to the word non-recurrent and read as the opposite at a glance. It now reads "recurrent (all delay not attributed to an event)". No data or colour changed. See Delay composition by year.

Why are we showing tttr value in here instead of something comparative to the 'all vehicle' (assumption) value? Can we show % for truck as well? ticket 2214481

Truck Travel Time Reliability (TTTR) is a ratio, the 95th-percentile truck travel time over the 50th. It is not a share of reliable person-miles, and no federal "% reliable for trucks" exists. Rather than invent one, TTTR was removed from the home in August 2026. It stays on the Reliability dashboard against its 2.00 target, beside the method note. See TTTR.

I wonder why we do not have an option to view all vs truck vs auto ticket 2214482 · ticket 2214508

Reliability is the federal page. LOTTR is defined on all vehicles and TTTR on trucks; those are the only federally reportable pairings. A class switch would invite reading an "auto LOTTR" as a federal figure, so it was not built there. The class is stated on each card instead. The Macro View in NPMRDS offers a traffic-type control for its analytical measures. See Federal PM3 and what this site publishes.

I think this is probably taking the worst LOTTR from AM, Midday, PM or weekend, but that's unclear. ticket 2194999

Correct. The map colours each segment by its worst-period LOTTR, the highest of the AM, Midday, PM and Weekend ratios. The bins are under 1.25, 1.25–1.49, 1.50–1.99, and 2.00 and above. The legend title now reads in full. There is no period picker. See Where travel is unreliable.

I clicked on several crashes to see the details and couldn't get any to show the map in the "location & affected segment" box ticket 2195391 · ticket 2196812

The map works; most events have nothing to draw on it. A footprint of affected segments exists on about one event in five. Since July 2026 the map always shows the event's location as a pin. For an event with no footprint it centres on the pin with no segment highlighted. A pin alone means no affected segments were recorded. See The map shows a pin but no segment.

Fixed in July 2026. On Data & Methodology, open data source → now leads to the delay-attribution source, which offers a CSV per year. The API card describes key-based access to the endpoint the dashboards read. See Downloads.

We say road segments fort the year but the events are …. All the years??? Why ticket 2214496

The home's Data Freshness band once counted every event in the whole multi-state feed beside a year-filtered segment count. Since August 2026 both counts follow the selected year and New York only. The dashboards' status lines still quote the whole feed, "3.7M events since 2015"; their panels follow Year. See Coverage and freshness.

Year selection – can we remove the partial year option ticket 2214476

The year chips offer every year the delay series holds, and some are partial. A partial year's total is a partial total, presented in the same card as a full year. Check the year before you quote it; 2025 is the latest complete year. See Partial years show partial totals.
