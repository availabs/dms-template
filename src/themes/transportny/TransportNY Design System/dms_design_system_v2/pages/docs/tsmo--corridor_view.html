Explore a corridor over time.

Replay one corridor through one day. Pick a county, a corridor, a direction and a date, read the time-space speed grid, see the day's events drawn over it, and check the strip map to confirm which segments you are looking at.

how-to applies to · TSMO (www/tsmo) last reviewed · 2026-09-05 owner · —

Before you start

Open Explorers › Corridor View. No account is needed. Speeds arrive in a daily batch about 2 weeks behind, so the most recent days are not there yet. The page is county-keyed: a region set on a dashboard does not follow you here.

Steps

  1. Open Corridor View.It opens on a default corridor with its grid already drawn.
  2. Pick the county.The corridor list narrows to that county's roads.
  3. Pick the corridor. A corridor is one road's chain of segments within the county.
  4. Pick the direction.
  5. Pick the date. Or use the month strip above the grid, Daily pattern & time-space speed. It plots each day's average speed for the month; selecting a day drives the grid.The grid refetches for that corridor and day.
  6. Read the grid. Each row is one segment in road order, labelled by its cross street where known, its height proportional to its length in miles. Each column is one 5-minute slice from 05:00 to midnight. Each cell is the average speed in mph: above 55, 45–55, 35–45, 25–35, below 25. A blank cell had no probe data.Slow cells stacked down a column are a queue at that time; slow cells across a row are a segment that ran slow all day.
  7. Read Incidents on this corridor: the TRANSCOM events touching the corridor that day, one row per segment, open to close. Their spans are drawn on the grid with an outline coloured by category: Crash, Work zone, Road hazard, Other.
  8. Read Corridor at a glance: the corridor's length, its traffic count, and the day's worst measured speed.
  9. Check Corridor strip map. It draws exactly the segments feeding the grid and zooms to them.If a segment looks wrong, you can see it here before you trust the grid.

Why the region selection does not carry into it

The dashboards filter by NYSDOT region. Corridor View filters by county, road and direction, and the network it reads carries no region for a segment. A region therefore cannot be translated into a corridor selection. That is why the Congestion dashboard's "drill into a corridor" button was removed in July 2026: it landed on the default county whatever region was set. Open Corridor View and pick the county yourself. Counties and their regions are listed in Geography.

Limits and gotchas

  • Speed is segment length divided by observed travel time. On very short segments this can imply impossible values, so speeds are capped at 80 mph.
  • The overnight slices from midnight to 05:00 are dropped as too sparse.
  • A blank cell is missing probe data, not a stopped road.
  • Corridors split at county lines. A long road is several corridors, one per county.
  • The home's explorer card promises comparing dates side by side. No compare control exists on the live page.
  • The grid's row labels are small on long corridors.

Troubleshooting

The grid is blank

The date is probably inside the 2-week lag. Pick an earlier day in the month strip. If an older day is blank, the corridor had no probe data that day.

My road is not in the corridor list

Check the county. Roads are listed per county, and the list only holds roads with monitored segments there.

The page ignored my region

It always will. See why the region selection does not carry into it.
