Start here

Each product has a guided first task of about 10 minutes. Pick the product that matches your question.

  • NPMRDS · you have a road or corridor in mind and want its speed, travel time or reliability over time. Build a route, put it in a report, read the measure. Saving the route and the report needs an account.
  • TSMO · you want to know how congestion, reliability, incidents or work zones looked in a year, statewide or in a NYSDOT region. Pick a year, read the three numbers, open a dashboard. No account needed.
  • Freight Atlas · you want a freight map from the 2024 State Freight Plan, or the data behind it. Open a gallery map, read its legend, share it. No account needed.

Common tasks

  • Sign in, and check whether your task needs an account.
