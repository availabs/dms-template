Start here

One guided task of about 10 minutes: open a map, read it, share it. You open the Maps Gallery, choose the plan's maritime system figure, read its legend, add a rail layer, switch a road layer's view, and copy the address that reproduces your layers. No account is needed.

If you are about to quote a number, read How the Freight Atlas relates to the plan first. The home's headlines are the plan's figures for the TRANSEARCH 2021 base year (2023 release). A layer's values are its dataset's, at the dataset's vintage.

Common tasks

  • Open one of the plan's maps from the Maps Gallery, and find out why a figure is not there yet.
  • Turn layers on and off, change the view a layer colours by, and deal with a layer that will not go.
  • Read a legend block and open the dataset behind it with the info button.
  • Identify a road at street zoom: shields and names come from the basemap.
  • Share a map by its address, knowing it carries layers but not position.
  • Look up what a layer shows and which dataset it draws, and what FCHN, PHFS, STRAHNET, STRACNET and DAC mean.
  • Download a dataset in CSV, Shapefile, GeoJSON or GeoPackage, and find out why the catalog asked you to sign in.
  • Read the plan documents at NYSDOT's reports page.
  • Compare the 2024 plan with the 2019 plan before comparing their numbers.
