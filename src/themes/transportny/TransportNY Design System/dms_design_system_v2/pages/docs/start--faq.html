Do I need an account?

Not to read. Every dashboard, map, published report and MAP-21 PM3 page opens without one. You need an account to save routes and reports, and to administer data: uploading datasets and building maps. The Data & Downloads catalog currently asks for sign-in by mistake. Full detail: Sign in, sessions and accounts.

Site keeps logging me out ticket 2214470

A session lasts about 6 hours. When it expires, the site returns you to the sign-in page. A second cause is moving to NPMRDS, which runs on its own subdomain and keeps its own session. Since September 2026, the landing page, TSMO and the Freight Atlas share one sign-in. If you are signed out repeatedly well inside 6 hours, report it with the time and page. See How long a session lasts.

Using links from main TransportNY still needs extra login. Also from TSMO to NPMRDS requires continuous login running fix 2172529

Fixed for two of the three products. Since September 2026 the landing page, TSMO and the Freight Atlas live on one origin, so one sign-in covers them. NPMRDS still runs on its own subdomain. Moving to it can ask you to sign in again, with the same email and password. See Troubleshooting sign-in.

left nav "platform home" option....what does that do??? ticket 2197822

Platform home is the last entry of the product switcher at the top of every side navigation. It returns you to the landing page, the front door where the three products are listed. Nothing is lost by selecting it. See Navigating the platform.

Docs page link goes to NPMRDS Docs? running fix 2172528

It used to. There is now one documentation site for all three products, sectioned by product, at /docs. The intended path is a Docs link in each product's navigation, which is not built yet. Until it is, add /docs to the site address. See Where the documentation is.

What is actually being shown when there is no filter on region? Is there anyway to clarify this? ticket 2191486

Statewide. With no region selected, a TSMO dashboard shows the full PM3 submission dataset, the same aggregate behind the federal figures. That is deliberately not the sum of the 11 regions. The submission includes border and unassigned segments that belong to no region, so the regions add up to less than statewide. The filter bar now says "no region selected = statewide", and clearing a region restores the statewide figures. See Geography: regions, counties, MPOs, statewide.

Why are we showing this on an annual basis for all years instead of the year selected???? ticket 2214490

Because the measure is annual. Level of Travel Time Reliability (LOTTR) is a federal MAP-21 measure defined on a calendar year. A monthly LOTTR does not exist in any data the platform holds. The reliability series therefore cannot follow the year selector month by month as the delay series does. The tile is labelled annual for that reason. See LOTTR.

Albany International Airport is not listed on the legend and not identified on the map. ticket 2192902

The Maps Gallery reproduces the figures of the 2024 State Freight Plan. The plan did not classify Albany International or Schenectady County as freight airports, so the Major Airports map follows the plan. See Maps Gallery and The 2024 State Freight Plan.

How do I report a problem or ask a question?

Use the platform's status pages at /status, which carry the bug report form and the list of known issues. The form is at /status/bug_report; known issues are listed at /status. Give the page address, the filters you had set, and what you expected. Once live, the Was this page helpful? buttons at the foot of every docs page also land in the same queue. See Help and feedback.

How can I find a route's recent behavior?

Open Reports in NPMRDS and start a report from a template on your route. Templates cover an average day, a week, a season, month by month, year over year, and before and after. If you have no saved route yet, create one first. See Start a report from a template and Template reference.

Can I analyze a route before and after a specific time period to evaluate a change?

Yes. In Reports, chart one route with a period before the change and a period after it. The recipe walks through a real project with the route and dates, so you can reproduce it. See Recipe: a before/after study.

How can I look at and compare a routes long-term behavior patterns?

Two tools. A year-over-year report in Reports charts one route across the years. Route Comparison puts several routes and several periods in one table with a base period. Each cell gives speed, travel time, delay and the percent change against the base. See Compare routes across periods.

Which segments have the highest amount of excessive delay?

Open the Macro View, choose a delay measure, set your geography, and open Worst 25 segments. Total Excessive Delay (TED) counts all hours of the day. Peak Hour Excessive Delay (PHED) counts the federally defined peak periods only. Check the no-data count first: delay needs traffic volume, and many segments have none. See Macro View: how to use.

Which segments have the highest amount of excessive delay during peak hours?

The same steps with PHED as the measure. Peak Hour Excessive Delay is the MAP-21 PM3 measure that counts delay inside the federal peak periods only. See PHED.

Which segments have the highest amount of excessive delay for Trucks?

In the Macro View, choose PHED. Its Traffic Type control offers All Traffic and All Trucks. Choose All Trucks, then open Worst 25 segments. For truck reliability rather than delay, choose TTTR. See Macro View: how to use.

Where are peak hour travel times most variable for commuters?

Open the Macro View with LOTTR, its default measure. Level of Travel Time Reliability is the 80th-percentile travel time divided by the 50th, for all vehicles in a peak period. Higher means less predictable. Pick the peak period, then read the map or Worst 25 segments. See LOTTR.

Where are peak hour travel times most variable for freight delivery?

The same view with TTTR. Truck Travel Time Reliability is the 95th-percentile truck travel time divided by the 50th. It flags the segments most likely to upset a delivery schedule. See TTTR.

Which segments have the highest amount of recurrent (regular/normal) delay?

Recurrent delay is the everyday pattern. Non-recurrent delay comes from incidents, weather and work zones. The split is on the TSMO Congestion dashboard, in its delay composition chart, by region and month. The Macro View's measure menu does not include it. See Congestion dashboard and Delay attribution.

I need to export data to perform analyses in different software.

Three routes. The Macro View download gives every segment in your geography as CSV or GPKG, with method notes. Data & Downloads in the Freight Atlas offers each dataset as CSV, GeoJSON, Shapefile, GeoPackage or XLSX. Developers can query the Data Manager API. See Download data from the Macro View, Download freight data and the Developers quick start.

I need to find the definitions or calculations associated with terminology found within these tools.

Measures & Data has one page per measure. Each says what it answers, how it is computed with every variable expanded, how much to trust it, and where it appears. The Glossary lists every term with the screen it appears on.
