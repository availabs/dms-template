How the products divide the questions

A road segment or a route over time is NPMRDS. A region's operations in a year, month by month, is TSMO. Freight networks, facilities and the 2024 State Freight Plan are the Freight Atlas.

Two rules before you quote a number

  • Federal is not analytical. Only the MAP-21 PM3 pages carry the figures NYSDOT submits to FHWA. Every other reliability number is the analytical series. See Federal PM3 and what this site publishes.
  • Download what you cite. A shared link is live and can change. A download is fixed and ships its method notes. See Share a page by its address.
