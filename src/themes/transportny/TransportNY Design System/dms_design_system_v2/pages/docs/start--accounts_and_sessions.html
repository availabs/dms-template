Sign in, and know what a session does.

Everything that reads is public. Sign in only to save routes and reports, or to administer data. Here are the sign-in steps, what needs an account, and why the site sometimes asks you to sign in again.

how-to applies to · all products last reviewed · 2026-09-04 owner · —

Before you start

Start from the landing page or any product page. To sign in you need an account: an email address and a password. Reading needs nothing. If your task is in a "No" row below, skip this page.

If you do not have an account, request one through Help and feedback.

Steps

  1. Open the sign-in page. Its address is /auth/login on any product.You see a form with Email and Password.
  2. Enter your email address in Email.
  3. Enter your password in Password. If you have forgotten it, use Forgot?. It opens the password reset page.
  4. Select Sign In.You are signed in. Saving routes and reports is now available.
  5. Move to TSMO or the Freight Atlas with the product switcher.You stay signed in. These products share one origin with the landing page.
  6. Move to NPMRDS. It runs on its own subdomain.You may be asked to sign in again. Use the same email and password.

How long a session lasts

A session lasts about 6 hours. When it expires, the site returns you to the sign-in page. Sign in again and continue. Anything you saved before the expiry is kept.

Since September 2026 the landing page, TSMO and the Freight Atlas share one origin, so one sign-in covers all three. NPMRDS still runs on its own subdomain with its own session, which can expire at a different time.

Limits and gotchas

  • Signing in does not change the numbers. Public and signed-in readers see the same data. Administrators see extra controls.
  • A session does not renew itself. Expect the sign-in page after about 6 hours, and save before long pauses.
  • The Data & Downloads catalog and the landing page's Data Sources entry ask visitors without an account to sign in. Both are meant to be public. This is a known issue.

Troubleshooting

The site keeps logging me out

Two causes are normal. Your session passed 6 hours, or you moved to NPMRDS, which has a separate session. Sign in again. If you are signed out repeatedly well inside 6 hours, report it with the time and the page.

I was asked to sign in again when I moved between products

Moving to NPMRDS asks again because it runs on its own subdomain. Moving between the landing page, TSMO and the Freight Atlas should not ask again. If it does, report it.

Data & Downloads asked me to sign in

This is the known issue above. Sign in to open the catalog. If you have no account, request one, or the file you need, through Help and feedback.
