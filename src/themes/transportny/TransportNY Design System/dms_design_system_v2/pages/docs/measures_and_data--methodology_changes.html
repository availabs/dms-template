Methodology changes.

Every dated change to the platform's numbers, methods, maps and pages, newest first. For anyone who quoted a figure and needs to know whether it still reproduces, and why not.

log applies to · all products last reviewed · 2026-09-05 owner · —

This log is the platform's change record. Each entry carries one of four labels. Platform: a page or control changed and no number moved. Data release: new months or a recomputation landed. Map/TMC version: the segment network changed vintage. Methodology: a definition or parameter changed, and figures computed before and after it are not comparable. Every measure and data page in this hub keeps its own Revision log. Those logs feed this one, and the page named in each entry holds the detail.

2026-09-02 · Platform

TSMO and the Freight Atlas moved onto the landing page's origin, at /tsmo and /freightatlas. One sign-in now carries across the products that share the origin. Before, each product was a separate session. NPMRDS keeps its own address until it is ready to move. No number moved. See Navigating the platform and Sign in, sessions and accounts.

2026-09-01 · Platform

The Route Creation page published with validated tags. An agency: and user: vocabulary with suggestion chips replaces free-text tags. A new route is seeded with the saver's own tags. See Routes data.

2026-08-28 · Methodology

The Measures & Methodology rewrite published. It adds measured precision bands to every measure and defines the nine coverage eras that govern comparability across years. It names two traps: a delay trend is partly an AADT revision history, and "PM peak" means two different windows. It also records that the travel-time feed arrives filtered upstream and that sub-second travel times are correct. No number moved. New figures were published beside the old. The measure pages in this hub were written from it. See Coverage eras and comparability, AADT and its vintage and Peak periods and epochs.

2026-08-25 · Platform

Two chart corrections. The TSMO Congestion composition chart's grey legend now reads "recurrent (all delay not attributed to an event)", after a reader took it for non-recurrent. The Freight Atlas home's mode-share chart now reads the 2024 State Freight Plan's own figures instead of deriving shares live. The Freight Atlas agrees with the plan by rule. See Delay attribution and Freight Atlas sources.

2026-08-24 · Platform

The TSMO home's reliability card split. Non-Interstate reliability got its own equal-weight card. TTTR was removed from the home because a unitless index had nothing comparable beside it. TTTR stays on the Reliability dashboard with its 2.00 target. See Reliability dashboard.

2026-08-23 · Methodology

The anchored free-flow reference window moved to June 2023 to July 2024 when the publish scope grew to the full 2017 to 2025 archive. The anchored PHED and TED thresholds moved with it. On identical CY 2025 data, floor included, the anchored variant yields 1.18 % more network delay than the own-year reference. See PHED.

2026-08-21 · Methodology

The anchored free-flow reference window moved from the era starting December 2024 to the era starting January 2021. The first choice contained all of CY 2025 and was therefore a no-op for that year. Anchored PHED and TED thresholds moved. Own-year figures did not. See PHED.

2026-08-18 · Platform

On the Macro View map the LOTTR target of 1.50 became a colour boundary, with no change to the computation. The Macro View's TTTR threshold was corrected from 1.50 to the 2.00 Interstate target. The share of segments above it changed from 74.1 % to 44.7 %. See LOTTR and TTTR.

2026-08-18 · Methodology

The analytical series gained its first additive columns. They are truck 80th over 50th percentile beside TTTR (TTTR₈₀), the delay-threshold diagnostics, per-row quality columns, precision bands and per-stream era tags. The anchored free-flow reference was published as a second delay variant beside the own-year one. Data coverage was published as a measure in the same month. No existing number moved. See TTTR₈₀, Data coverage and PM3 and MAP-21 datasets.

2026-08-14 · Methodology

The analytical series forked from the MAP-21 code. The federal computation is frozen so the submittal figures stay compliant and reproducible. The analytical series can now change its definitions without touching them. No number moved on the day. Every later Methodology entry applies to the analytical series only. See Federal PM3 and what this site publishes and PM3 and MAP-21 datasets.

2026-07-31 · Methodology

The Work Zones dashboard's methodology band published, with the exposure and safety measures marked by status. See Work-zone delay.

2026-07-28 · Platform

Incidents and Work Zones stopped overlapping. Events whose NYSDOT sub-category is Construction, Maintenance or Emergency Operations belong to Work Zones. Incidents keeps the reactive set, and its Category filter no longer offers Construction. The "active work zones" section was removed. Dashboard counts changed. The underlying store did not. See Incidents dashboard and Work Zones dashboard.

2026-07-27 · Data release

The TSMO Data & Methodology page published the gap in the excessive-delay series. The current run covers 2018 and 2021 to 2025. The years 2019 and 2020 are not computed. It also published the refresh-cadence table. TRANSCOM events are continuous and travel times a daily batch about 2 weeks behind. Delay attribution is a batch recompute with a monthly target, and PM3 measures are annual on the federal cycle. See Excessive-delay series and Coverage and freshness.

2026-07-27 · Platform

The Congestion dashboard's "drill into a corridor" link was removed. Congestion filters by NYSDOT region and Corridor View is county-keyed, so the drill could not carry the selection through. A neutral link to the corridor explorer replaces it. See Congestion dashboard.

2026-07-20 · Platform

Every TSMO filter control gained a visible border, and panel titles follow the selected region where some had been fixed text. In the same month Region became single-select on Congestion. It still accepts several regions on Reliability, Incidents and Work Zones. See Using the filter bar.

2026-07-19 · Map/TMC version

The platform's current network moved to the 2026 map, 52,473 segments, as the travel-time data ran into 2026. Per-segment figures for 2026 are computed on this map. Earlier years keep their own. See TMC network and The TMC network and its vintages.

2026-07-15 · Data release

June 2026 landed in the excessive-delay series, the first forward monthly run after the June restart. It attributed 36,574 New York events and extended the event-by-segment table through June. The run confirmed that 2017 was never computed. See Excessive-delay series and TRANSCOM events.

2026-06-22 · Methodology

The class-weighted value of time was confirmed as version 1: $52 per passenger-vehicle hour, $42 per single-unit-truck hour, $77 per combination-truck hour. The rate is applied at the data layer from each segment's AADT split. Recomputation of the published series was pending, so pages show two bases during the transition. See Cost of congestion: which pages show which basis.

2026-06-22 · Data release

Timestamp completeness was measured on 3.7 million TRANSCOM events and published. All lanes reopened is present on 42.7 % of events and incident verified on 20.1 %. Incident reported is present on 10.6 % and response on scene on 0.3 %. Clearance time therefore exists for about one event in five. See TRANSCOM events.

2026-06-19 · Methodology

The class-weighted value of time was adopted to replace the flat $20 per vehicle-hour, and the occupancy double-count was removed. The network blend is about $50 to $55 per vehicle-hour, roughly 2.5 times the old rate. Every dollar figure recomputed on the new basis rises by that factor. See Cost of congestion.

2026-06-13 · Data release

The Congestion dashboard's data revision on the restarted series. CY 2025, statewide, all vehicles: 310.86 million vehicle-hours of excessive delay, 157.07 million of them non-recurrent (51 %). Construction accounts for 46.84 million and accidents for 4.82 million. The cost is $6.2 billion at the $20 per vehicle-hour basis then in force. Years available at the revision: 2024 (November and December, partial), 2025, and 2026 (January to April, partial). See Congestion dashboard and Excessive delay.

2026-06-10 · Methodology

The excessive-delay revision. The threshold became the greater of 20 mph or 60 % of the posted speed limit, with posted limits live per segment. The attribution baseline became the median, and attribution was capped so an event cannot claim more delay than exists. The series was restarted and refilled from the newest complete month backwards. Figures published before this date do not compare with figures after it. See Excessive delay and Delay attribution.

2026-02 · Data release

The CY 2025 MAP-21 dataset was computed on the federal cycle. It is the frozen federal computation, not the analytical series. See PM3 and MAP-21 datasets.

2025 · Map/TMC version

The 2025 network holds 52,157 segments. The day the platform adopted it is not recorded. The count was verified on 2026-07-29. See TMC network.

2024 · Map/TMC version

The 2024 network holds 52,029 segments. The day the platform adopted it is not recorded. The count was verified on 2026-07-29. See TMC network.
