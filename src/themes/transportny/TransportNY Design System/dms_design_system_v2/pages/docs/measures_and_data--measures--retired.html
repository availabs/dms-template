Measures that used to be here.

The 2024 documentation listed measures and attributes the current tools no longer offer. If you relied on one, this page says what happened to it, what to use instead, and where the capability went. Written for readers of the earlier NPMRDS documentation.

reference applies to · NPMRDS (www/npmrds) last reviewed · 2026-09-05 owner · —

What changed

The 2024 documentation was a federal PM3 primer with a "Using the Tools" half written for an older Macro tool. Its measure list came from that tool's catalogue. The 2026 rebuild replaced the tool with the Macro View and added TTTR₈₀, data coverage and the delay threshold variants. It dropped three index measures and a set of roadway attributes. Nothing was silently renumbered. Each retired item below says where it went.

Retired index measures

Three reliability indices from the FHWA reliability primer were listed and are not currently computed. Each compares a travel time to a free-flow travel time.

Planning Time Index (PTI)
The 95th-percentile travel time divided by the free-flow travel time. A PTI of 1.60 means planning 60 % extra time to arrive on time 19 trips in 20.
Buffer Index (BI)
The 95th-percentile travel time minus the mean travel time, divided by the mean. The extra share of time to add to an average trip.
Travel Time Index (TTI)
The mean travel time divided by the free-flow travel time. How much longer an average trip takes than an uncongested one.
MeasureStatusWhat to use instead
Planning Time IndexNot currently computedPercentile speed gives the 95th and 50th percentiles of the same distribution. Reinstating the index family is under evaluation
Buffer IndexNot currently computedLOTTR asks the same question with the federal percentile pair, 80th over 50th
Travel Time IndexNot currently computedPercentile speed at the 50th and 85th percentiles · TED for the volume-weighted time lost

Why they went: the indices need a free-flow travel time per segment. The platform's free-flow reference is not yet published as a measure of its own. See free-flow speed under Measures in development. The underlying distribution is what percentile speed shows directly.

Retired roadway attributes

The 2024 tool could colour the map by fields from NYSDOT's Roadway Inventory System (RIS). The current Macro View does not expose them.

AADT variants
Annual average daily traffic (AADT) by year and by vehicle class. AADT still enters every delay figure as the volume term; see TED.
Posted speed
The posted speed limit. It now sets the threshold in the posted-limit delay variant and in the TSMO excessive-delay series.
DDHV, adjusted rated capacity, volume-to-capacity ratio
Directional design-hour volume, the segment's capacity after adjustment, and the ratio of the two. Not shown anywhere on the platform today.
K and D factors
The share of AADT that falls in the design hour, and the share of design-hour traffic in the peak direction. Not shown anywhere on the platform today.

Where they went: the Attributes measure, declared but not yet computed, is the intended home for colouring the map by a network field. Until it exists, AADT and functional class ride along in every Macro View download, and HPMS / RIS attributes documents the source fields.

Restored under a new name

Percent bins reporting is back as Data coverage. It is now published per stream (all vehicles or trucks) and per period, with a second figure, epochs reporting, beside it. One correction came with the restoration. The bin count now counts bins where the stream had a value, not bins where the feed had any row. The older count had inflated the truck figure roughly two times.
