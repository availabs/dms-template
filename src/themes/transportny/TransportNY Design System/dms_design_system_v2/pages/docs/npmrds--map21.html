MAP-21 PM3 reporting pages.

The federal reliability, freight and delay figures New York reports to FHWA, by calendar year, scored against the statewide targets. Two pages are live: the MAP-21 PM3 report and its Level of Travel Time Reliability page. This page says how to read them, what to cite, and where the annual submission file is. Reading is public.

how-to applies to · NPMRDS last reviewed · 2026-09-07 owner · —

Before you start

Open MAP-21 PM3 from the NPMRDS side navigation. No sign-in is needed. These pages show the federal family: Level of Travel Time Reliability (LOTTR), Truck Travel Time Reliability (TTTR) and Peak Hour Excessive Delay (PHED) as 23 CFR 490 defines them, computed on the platform from the MAP-21 dataset. Every other tool shows the analytical series; Federal PM3 and what this site publishes tells them apart.

Steps

  1. Open MAP-21 PM3.The first band is Compliance snapshot · CY 2025.
  2. Pick a year with the Year selector.Every card, chart and table recomputes for that year.
  3. Read the LOTTR and TTTR cards against their targets: a status pill, a target bar and the change from the year before. The PHED card is context; its target is per urbanized area.
  4. Read the trend chart under each of the four measure headings.
  5. Read How MAP-21 targets work before calling a figure "met". The rules are tabled below.
  6. Read the Regional · MPO table, one row per Metropolitan Planning Organization (MPO) against the statewide target, and Urban congestion · CMAQ · per UZA, the two reporting urbanized areas against their own.
  7. For the submission file, set Year and use the download under Annual data · HPMS Travel Time Metrics.
  8. Open the Level of Travel Time Reliability page for the map, distribution and least-reliable segments. Its address is /map_21/level_of_travel_time_reliability, a child of the MAP-21 page.
  9. Cite a figure with its measure, year, system, target and basis: "83.2 % of non-Interstate NHS person-miles reliable in CY 2025, against a 70 % target, computed on the platform; MAP-21 PM3 page, read 2026-09-07".

A segment is reliable when the worst of its four federal periods, weekday AM, midday, PM and the weekend, has a LOTTR below 1.50. The percentage is the share of person-miles on reliable segments, each weighted by length, directional AADT and occupancy, so a long, busy segment moves it more than a short, quiet one. LOTTR and TTTR define the ratios.

The TSMO home quotes 80.0 %, 83.3 % and 1.46 for CY 2025, from the file New York resubmitted to FHWA. Both are the federal measure; cite the value the page you point at shows and name its basis. PM3 and MAP-21 datasets owns the explanation.

RuleWhat the page says
Significant progressA two-prong test. A measure passes if it meets the 2-year or 4-year target, or if it improved on the period's baseline year while short of target (TTTR by at least 0.01). Statuses: met target · significant progress · not meeting
Performance periodsFour years with a 2-year midpoint check. Period 2 is 2022 to 2025; period 3 is 2026 to 2029
MPO adoptionWithin 180 days of the State setting a target, each MPO supports it or sets its own. New York's MPOs support the statewide targets
Urban congestionPHED and Non-SOV apply only to urbanized areas over 200,000 people in nonattainment or maintenance: New York–Newark and Poughkeepsie–Newburgh. The other 11 New York urbanized areas do not report them

The annual download

The file under Annual data · HPMS Travel Time Metrics is New York's Highway Performance Monitoring System (HPMS) Travel Time Metrics submission for the selected year: one row per reporting TMC segment, both directions, with the per-period LOTTR and TTTR ratios and their percentile travel times, PHED hours, and the segment's system, length and directional AADT. It is filed with FHWA each June 15 under 23 CFR 490; the Year selector switches the file. The band's text reads "Download the year's submission file". The button reads Download CSV; at the 2026-09-08 check its address was a placeholder that did not resolve, so the file is not yet served. The datasheet's schema lists the columns. This file, not a Macro View download, reproduces a federal figure.

Limits and gotchas

  • Targets exist at two geographies, the State and the urbanized area. An MPO row is scored against the statewide target it adopted; a county has no federal target, so county figures are diagnostic.
  • PHED's target is per capita; the dataset holds total person-hours, so the comparison needs the area's population.
  • The pages cover the NHS. Macro View counts cover every monitored segment and are not comparable.
  • The period 3 (2026 to 2029) targets are not on the pages.
  • The header promises an MPO and County breakdown; the live table is by MPO. The LOTTR map has one layer and no period control.
  • Older handoff copy carried 85.0 % and 1.42 for CY 2025; neither reproduces from any dataset, and both are withdrawn.

Troubleshooting

The figure differs from the TSMO home

The home quotes the FHWA resubmission; these pages compute from the MAP-21 dataset. The gap is 0.2 and 0.1 points on LOTTR, none on TTTR.

A year shows empty cards

The dataset holds calendar years 2016 to 2025. Pick 2025; if that is blank too, report it.

The LOTTR map has no colour

Wait for the tiles. Grey is No data, not zero.
