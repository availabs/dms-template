A measure brings its own controls.

Three controls are always on — geography, the view mode, and the year. Everything else belongs to the measure and sits directly under the measure selector, so choosing a measure and tuning it happen in one place rather than at two ends of the panel. Traffic type is not global: only the two delay measures expose it, which is why it lives inside the measure block.
