MitigateNY · Plan Administration

Sullivan County

This is the administration side of your hazard mitigation plan — the working data behind the public plan site.

Your plan is written from six datasets that you and your jurisdictions maintain here. Editing a record updates the published plan directly, so the plan and the data never drift apart. Nothing you change is public until the plan is republished. Your changes are scoped to this county — statewide catalogs are maintained by DHSES.

How your plan comes together

Every Local Hazard Mitigation Plan is built from the same four elements, fixed by 44 CFR 201.6. Each one is written from the datasets named beneath it.
