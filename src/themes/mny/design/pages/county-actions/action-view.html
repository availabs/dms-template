Delaware — Kohlertown Route 52, Culvert Issues

Delaware (Town) · Sullivan County · Action 2026-DelawareT-03 · Local plan
Back to worklist Edit action
In-Progress HMP Review Ready Local priority High Status updated 1 May 2026 County priority not set — assign a tier

The case for this action

The problem

State Route 52 in Kohlertown experiences severe flooding on a continuous basis due to culvert issues and requires coordination of various agencies responsible for transportation services.

Undersized culverts often result in the flooding of roadways due to the inability to handle the influx of water. Debris build-up in these undersized pipes may also result in water back-flow, leading to further roadway flooding and impacting the integrity of the culverts.

The solution

To reduce the long-term risk to flooding for existing assets and future developments, specifically along State Route 52 and adjacent roads in Kohlertown near border of Jeffersonville/Callicoon, the Town Highway Department will work with County Public Works and NYS DOT to upsize of two culverts and an associated stormwater drain to increase stormwater drainage capacity. The state Department of Transportation is currently replacing one culvert, and the county Department of Public Works is working on an overflow storage or redirection solution.

Elevate roads Rejected — cost prohibitive

Why it pays off
Overall flooding will be reduced, which will result in less frequency of road closures and reduced damage occurring to culverts and roadways during severe events. Businesses are likely to remain in place if they are able to remain open, or re-open sooner following a flood.
