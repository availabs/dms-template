WestchesterBronxville (Village) Alder Lane Flooding Community Infrastructure In-Progress Engineer, DPW $2M – $10M High

Description of Problem

During heavy rain events including storms and hurricanes, homeowners along Alder Lane, Dusenberry Road, Forest Lane, and Ridgecroft Road flood. Specifically, repetitive damage has occurred at 35 Alder Lane, 30 Forest Lane, 39 Dusenberry Road, 40 Dusenberry Road, 35 Ridgecroft Road.

Description of Solution

The Village has retained PCI Engineering to design plans which include new flood channels in existing open space between Middle Road and Crawford Street as well as between Grove Street and Crawford Street, a storm water retention pond within Crawford Park, and backflow devices on the existing Dusenberry Road line…
