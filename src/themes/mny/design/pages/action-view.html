Description of the Problem (Problem Statement)

County Road 14 floods during moderate storm events, cutting off emergency access to Bloomingburg and surrounding hamlets for up to 18 hours at a time.

Description of the Solution (Action Description)

Raise a 0.6-mile segment of County Road 14 above the base flood elevation, with upsized cross-culverts to accommodate projected peak flows.
