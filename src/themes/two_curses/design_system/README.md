# Two Curses — design system (v0.13, 2026-09-06)

**Two Curses is a band** (Albany, NY); the site is its emblem-book. The look of a 17th-century emblem book: cream laid paper, black engraved plates, red rubrication for initials and emphasis, Fell types and Garamond. Built for the songbook at songs.b3nson.net (DMS app `asm`, site `nhomb`, pattern `songs`; the songs live in dataset source 1066383).

Shaped by `src/dms/skills/designing-a-dms-design-system.md`. Plain HTML + Tailwind CDN + `_shared.css`, no build step — serve the folder and open any page:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8765 --directory src/themes/two_curses/design_system
# → http://127.0.0.1:8765/pages/home.html
```

### v0.2 — what changed and why

The first pass read too "web": a boxed leaf with a double rule, hairline dividers, bordered cards, a max-width column. Version 0.2 removes every straight rule (the only lines left are dotted leaders and the dotted search underline), lets the page run the full viewport width, and moves the structure into the engravings: a headpiece opens a section, a tailpiece closes one, a frame holds a title.

## Assets

- Source scans are no longer kept under `assets/`: the full 1886 book lives in `references/quarles-emblems-1886/` (see below) and the original 32 downsized references in `references/two curses/`.
- `assets/alpha/` — every plate as an ink-only transparent PNG: alpha = darkness against the page's own paper level, paper grain zeroed, crop bounded by an eroded mask so scanner dust can't stretch it. These are what the pages show.
- `assets/frames/` + `assets/pictures/` — for the 16 plates whose picture sits in a straight-ruled window, the frame with the window cleared and the picture alone. `frames/windows.json` records each window as inset percentages; `_shared.css` turns those into `.tc-framed--NNNN` classes (+3% breathing room) so text can be set inside a frame.
- `assets/ornaments/` — 10 head- and tailpieces cropped from the Quarles plates (ink only, 2×).
- `assets/paper/` — the page ground, cut from the scans (v0.3). `grain.jpg` is a seamless 1024px quilt: high-passed patches from the blank margins of every Quarles plate, Hann cross-faded with wrap-around, laid on the sampled paper colour; tiled at 800px so the grain runs a little finer than the 300-dpi source. The browning (v0.5) is three document-anchored layers built from the Quarles leaves' paper colour *as a function of position*: every plate's paper pixels binned onto a 22×32 grid of the leaf and averaged, the bins under the pictures filled from their neighbours, the field blended 60/40 with its vertical flip (the scans' head is heavier than any real reader sees), divided by the centre colour so it can only darken and brown, raised to the power 1.6 and floored at 80%. From that field: `edges.jpg` is the side browning, repeated down the page; `top.jpg` and `bottom.jpg` are the head and foot bands, pinned to the ends of the document, with the side component divided out so corners aren't browned twice and forced to plain white at their inner edge so they join the grain seamlessly. All three multiply over the grain, all scroll with the document (the whole page is one sheet, not a window onto one), and their depth scales with viewport width, not page length. No mobile override is needed any more.
- **Resolution caveat:** the scans are ~400 × 550 px once trimmed, so a plate shown at 600 px is upscaled about 1.5× and softens slightly. Higher-resolution downloads of the same Internet Archive items would fix this without any other change.
- Regeneration: the PIL pipeline (alpha → window detection → frames/pictures → CSS classes) was run inline in the session; if crops need adjusting, the logic is simple enough to re-create from the description above, and the ten round-frame and text-page false positives it produces must be dropped by name.

## The full Quarles book (v0.6)

The plates in `references/two curses/` were 551×795 viewer downsizes. Their source is Internet Archive item **`quarlesemblems00quaruoft`** — *Quarles' Emblems*, London: James Nisbet & Co., 1886, illustrated by Charles H. Bennett and W. Harry Rogers, engraved by Swain and Evans; scanned by the University of Toronto at **400 ppi**, 346 leaves at **2202 × 3178 px** (4× what we had). Two other scans exist, both of the 1861 first printing at 500 ppi: `quarlesemblemsil00quar` (Library of Congress, 2562 × 3976) and `quarlesemblems00quar` (Illinois). The 1886 Toronto copy is the one to use: our leaf numbers, paper colour measurements, and existing crops all come from it.

The whole book now lives, gitignored, in **`references/quarles-emblems-1886/`**:

- `pages/` — all 346 leaves decoded from the Archive's processed JP2 zip (129 MB) to JPEG, 334 MB.
- `library/` — every non-text engraving on every leaf, cut as an ink-only alpha PNG at full resolution, classified by size and shape and by the Archive's OCR layer (a component overlapping OCR words is text; plates are allowed a few motto words). Counts after cleanup: **90 plates** (Books I–III: scrolled rectangular frames with a windowed woodcut, 56 with a detected window → `frames/` + `pictures/`; Books IV–V: round medallions with head- and tailpieces), **79 cartouches** (the engraved "EMBLEM I." title scrolls, four styles, one per emblem — ideal for the poem numerals once the lettering is cleared), **134 ornaments** (58 head/tailpieces from text leaves, 76 split off the medallion plates by their empty rows or stalks, 2 cut by hand on leaf 202), 2 initials. Where a leaf yielded two plate-class components (frame and inner picture separately), the second is suffixed `_1`. `index.json` lists each item with leaf, kind, pixel size, page box, OCR word count and, for windowed plates, the inset percentages. `sheets/*.jpg` are contact sheets for browsing, labelled by leaf number.
- Pipeline lessons: name every component uniquely (frame and picture on one leaf once collided under the leaf name); plates and their head/tailpieces merge under a 40px dilation and are re-split afterwards (`split_plates.py`, separator = a row with no ink or only a <12%-wide stalk); the OCR layer's leaf names must be matched by name, not offset (six leaves are missing from it).
- Regenerate with the session's `process_book.py` (partitioned: `python3 process_book.py <root> <djvu.xml> <k> <n>` per worker, then `merge_book.py <root>`); `publish_assets.py <library> <design_system>` copies the items the pages use into `assets/` at 1200 px under the existing filenames and rewrites the `.tc-framed--NNNN` CSS from the full-resolution windows; `grain_hd.py` re-quilts the paper grain from the 400-ppi margins.

**Working set (v0.6):** the pages now use the full-resolution library — plates 22, 38, 334 whole; 26 and 146 as frames (26's picture also shown alone); eight head/tailpieces — published by `publish_assets.py` as 8-bit grey+alpha PNGs at ≤1200 px (plates, frames) and ≤900 px (ornaments, pictures), 11 MB in all; everything else was pruned from `assets/`. The paper grain is re-quilted from the 400-ppi margins (2048 px tile shown at 1024).

**Picking assets:** browse `library/sheets/`, note the leaf number, and copy the item into `assets/` at ≤1200 px (plates, frames), ≤900 px (ornaments, pictures). Keep `assets/` to what the pages use — the library is the reservoir, the repo carries only the working set.

## Page frame and cartouche headings (v0.7)

**The whole home page is framed** by the Book I plate frame (leaf 26). It is Layout chrome: `.tc-framed-page` on the Layout outerWrapper adds padding for the frame, and a `.tc-pageframe` element inside it positions twelve pieces cut from the frame (`assets/pageframe/`):

- four **L-shaped corners cut long in both directions**, each carrying everything the plate attaches to it: TERRA with the crest's left dragon along the top (cut where its snout meets the shield's scroll) and the side dragon below; AER with the right dragon and the rooster below; IGNIS with the ribbon's left flourish along the foot and the dragon standing on the flames; AQUA with the right flourish and the rooster on the jug. On the plate these all touch, so those joins are the engraving's own;
- the whole **dragon crest** top-centre as well (so dragons flank the corners and guard the shield — three pairs along a wide top) and the **lettered ribbon and basket** bottom-centre. The corner dragons are separated from the shield not by a cut line but by subtracting the shield piece's own pixels, so each snout stays whole;
- between them, **thorn-vine runs**: a segment of clean thorn that starts where the upper corner leaves off (the corners were extended to take the flower-buds, so the segment holds no creature), followed by its own reflection, tiled (`repeat-y`, and rotated for the top and bottom gaps). A mirrored tile is seamless with itself. **Three figures per side, placed as the plate places them.** The upper corners are cut long enough to carry their figures — the dragon hangs under the TERRA scroll, the rooster under AER, exactly as engraved — and the lower corners carry theirs standing on the flames and the jug. A single middle figure per side (the other creature, flipped to face inward, its cut ends faded) makes three; it hides via a container query on pages shorter than 1500 px. The runs are plain mirrored thorn, split into an upper half anchored to the top corner and a lower half anchored to the bottom, so both corner joins are tidy and the two halves dissolve into the middle figure. Earlier passes tried figures baked into the repeating tile (regular, then irregular); at page length that was simply too many creatures — two or three a side is the right density. An earlier tile whose segment ended at the dragon's crown mirrored two crowns nose to nose; the lesson is that the mirrored segment must contain no part of a creature. **Joins are cross-dissolves, not cuts:** the corners and side figures are cut 70 source px past their edge with the ink fading out over that span, and the runs extend under them and fade in over the same span (a CSS `mask` gradient). The crest and ribbon are different: each is cut at its natural extent (x 290–1320, where the dragons' tail scrolls and the ribbon's flourishes actually end) with only a 24 px fade, because those ends are thin and complete in themselves — cutting them at the corner column, as the first pass did, lost the tail tips and the ribbon ends. The top member has no plain stretch — the corner scrolls run straight into the dragons — so a hard cut could never match; a dissolve inside a thorn tangle reads as more tangle. The top and bottom runs sit on the corners' horizontal members (135 / 120 source px in from the edge), with the crest and ribbon overhanging them as on the plate. This keeps the frame's uniqueness (no motif repeats except the plain thorn) while fitting any page length or width.
- One variable, `--pf` (side thickness, 150px desktop / 56px mobile), scales every piece from the source thickness of 330px; head and foot padding follow the crest and ribbon heights. On mobile the same pieces simply scale down. Piece widths in the CSS are the crops' real source widths (690 / 672 / 545 / 527) — a rounded width scales a piece against its run and shows as a step at the join.
- To frame another page, add `tc-framed-page` to its Layout div and paste the `.tc-pageframe` block from `home.html`. To use a different plate frame, cut its pieces the same way (corner / crest / ribbon / features / one plain segment per side) — the CSS only needs the source thickness and piece heights.
- AI in-painting was considered for the fills and not used: the reflected-segment approach keeps every mark the engraver's, and the thorn vine tolerates reflection well. Frames whose sides carry figures rather than vines would need a different fill strategy.

**Cartouches as headings.** `library/cartouches-clear/` holds 76 of the book's 79 engraved "EMBLEM N." title scrolls with the lettering removed (letters are the small ink components isolated inside the panel; two script banners, leaves 19 and 147, could not be cleared this way and are left as they are). Each has a measured text window (grown from the panel centre until it meets ink) recorded in `index.json` under `clear`. Three are published to `assets/cartouches/` with `.tc-framed--cNNNN` window rules: 0275 (Book V) carries the emblem numeral on the poem page, 0151 (Book III) carries "The Emblems" on the home page, 0085 (Book II) is ready. The four styles map naturally onto the book's five sections if the songbook ever wants that structure.

## Page framing — tried and set aside (v0.7–v0.9)

Two whole-page framings were built and both were removed on 2026-09-04; the home page is unframed again, with the thorn-and-dragon frame back around its title only.

- **Scrolling frame** (`.tc-framed-page` + `.tc-pageframe`, pieces in `assets/pageframe/`): the Book I frame cut into L-shaped corners carrying their figures, the crest and ribbon as centrepieces, plain mirrored-thorn runs with one middle figure per side, all joins either the plate's own or cross-dissolves. It works mechanically and the CSS and pieces are kept as a kit, but it is on no page.
- **The leaf** (frame uncut, fitted to the screen with the page scrolling inside its window): tried at viewport height, at 1040 px, and at full width. Removed entirely — the plate's proportions never suited a screen: fitted to height the window was 400 px wide; at full width the frame was 2000 px tall for one column of text.

The lesson for the theme: these frames were drawn for a page you hold, at a fixed ratio. On screen they belong around **things** — a title, a cartouche heading, a plate — not around the page.

## Ink (v0.12)

The plates are worn grey-brown ink; flat black vector type sat on them like a different medium. Two corrections, both in `_shared.css`:

- **Lighter, warmer ink tokens.** `ink` is `#3A3129` (was `#1E1B16`), `ink-soft` `#5A4E42` (was `#4A4238`); `sepia` and `rubric` unchanged.
- **Textured ink clipped to the glyphs.** Every text colour utility (`text-ink`, `text-ink-soft`, `text-sepia`, `text-rubric`) is overridden under `.tc-paper` to `color: transparent` with a tiled ink texture as `background-image` and `background-clip: text`. The textures (`assets/ink/*.png`, 512 px, shown at 256 so the flecks are sub-pixel-fine on retina) are the token colour with fine speckle and sparse pale wear where the paper shows through — the look of an impression from a forme, not a fill. Hover states swap the texture rather than the colour. Pseudo-elements (`::first-letter` drop caps, `::before` fleurons) cannot clip, so they keep plain rubric; form inputs keep plain ink.
- Translation note: `background-clip: text` is per-element, so in the live theme this belongs on the text-colour utilities exactly as here — a Tailwind plugin or `@utility` definitions for the four colours — rather than on containers.

## Decisions worth knowing

- **Full width, centered content.** No SideNav, no max-width cap: every LayoutGroup `wrapper2` is `w-full px-6 md:px-12 xl:px-20`. Narrow things (a framed title, the colophon) center themselves with `md:col-span-6 md:col-start-4` / `md:col-span-8 md:col-start-3` spans on the 12-column grid, not with a wrapper cap. Document as the brand's rule in `layouts.html`.
- **No rules.** LayoutGroup variants `header`, `content`, `footer` are all unboxed; there is no boxed variant. Dividers are ornaments (a section is a `tc-ornament` Section of its own on the grid). Buttons are small-caps text links with a rubric fleuron (`.tc-fleuron`), Pills are middot-separated small-caps links, active nav is the fleuron, not an underline.
- **`.tc-framed`** (and the cartouche variant) composes a Card image cell (the frame) with a text cell (the window) via absolute insets; **`.tc-pageframe`** is unused Layout chrome kept as a kit. These are the only places the mockups position anything absolutely; it is a documented brand surface, not per-page art, and the translation skill will need a Card variant or a small component for it.
- **Grid:** 12 columns, `gap-0`, section gutter `p-3`. Card cells use the same grid.
- **Paper colour.** `vellum` is `#FCF7E4`: the measured centre paper of the Quarles leaves (`#F9F2D0`) lifted 42% toward white on 2026-09-06 because the scans' cream read too yellow on screen; the margins measured about `#F7E9C0` and the head of the leaf `#E9D6AF`, and the edge layers still reproduce that gradient relative to the new base rather than a generic vignette. An earlier attempt that took a single scan's luminance and deepened it went orange-brown at the corners; the position-averaged colour field is what holds the look.
- **Type:** 13 tokens (`blackletterXL/MD`, `displayHero/XL/LG/MD`, `displayItalicMD/SM`, `proseLG/prose/proseSM`, `metaMD/SM`) — see `_shared.css` header. Small caps come from IM Fell English SC, so meta text is written lowercase, never `uppercase`.
- **Icons used so far:** Search, User, Menu, ArrowRight, ArrowLeft, Play, MusicalNote (Heroicons outline, inline, tagged `<!-- icon: Name -->`).
